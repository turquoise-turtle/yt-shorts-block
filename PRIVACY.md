# Privacy Policy

**Shorts Block** does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data.

The extension runs only on `www.youtube.com` and `m.youtube.com`. It reads the address of the page you are on and the layout of the YouTube page solely to decide whether a YouTube Short is open, and then hides it. This information never leaves your device: it is not sent to any server, not shared with any third party, and not written to any external storage.

The extension does not store anything at all. It does not write to `chrome.storage`, cookies, or any other browser storage. Its only permission, `storage`, is used solely to read settings that your school or organisation's administrator applies through managed policy. Those settings are only read, never changed, and never leave your device.

If an administrator turns on the redirect setting, opening a YouTube Short takes you to the address they chose instead. The extension sends no information with that navigation.

The extension does not use analytics, crash reporting, advertising, or any form of remote logging.

**In short:** your data stays on your device. The extension has no back-end and makes no network requests of its own.
