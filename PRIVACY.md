# Privacy Policy

**Shorts Block** does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data.

The extension runs only on `www.youtube.com` and `m.youtube.com`. It reads the address of the page you are on and the layout of the YouTube page solely to decide whether a YouTube Short is open, and then hides it. This information never leaves your device: it is not sent to any server, not shared with any third party, and not written to any external storage.

The extension does not store anything at all. It does not use `chrome.storage`, cookies, or any other browser storage, and it requests no browser permissions.

The extension does not use analytics, crash reporting, advertising, or any form of remote logging.

**In short:** your data stays on your device. The extension has no back-end and makes no network requests.
